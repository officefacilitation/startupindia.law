DIRECTOR

Aashish (Aash) Gupta

Techno-legal counsel and investment advisor with nearly two decades across technology, law, and venture finance.

Aashish began his career on a path toward Silicon Valley and a US Green Card, and chose instead to give both up and build an independent techno-legal practice in India: a decision that has shaped the firm's cross-border, technically fluent approach ever since.

He served as Director, IP Investment & Advisory Arm of Pendrell Corporation (NASDAQ: PCO) in Silicon Valley from 2012 to 2015, where he ran licensing programs covering roughly half the global mobile phone market and led due diligence on the acquisition of Nokia's memory business unit in Helsinki.

Separately from that role, he has advised on valuation strategy in the Apple v. Samsung patent dispute.

He has served as chief counsel to several Shark Tank-backed ventures, some now generating ₹100+ crore in revenue, and has worked alongside technologists connected to ISRO, DRDO, and BEL-affiliated programs, as well as IIT Bombay, GTU, SINE, and SocialAlpha.

He has held roles at Krishna & Saurastri Associates LLP (Partner) and Khaitan & Co. (Senior Associate, among the youngest to hold the title).

He has spoken at UC Berkeley Law, Government Law College Mumbai, IIT Bombay, GTU, and GIIP Delhi, has been an invited expert at Google advising leading YouTube creators, and mentors founders globally through the Techstars network.
